Our geospatial team has mapped five dam-release flood events in Kwara State
using Sentinel-1 radar imagery processed in Google Earth Engine. The figures
are now on our [Our Works](/projects) page, where each plate opens full size.

## What the maps show

Radar sees through cloud, which matters during the rainy season when optical
imagery is unusable. Comparing backscatter before and after each release
separates flood water from dry ground, and the resulting flood masks were
overlaid on cropland, population and local government boundaries.

- Inundation grew from 7 km² in October 2018 to a peak of 255 km² in
  September 2022.
- Flooded cropland rose with every event, reaching 122 km² in April 2025.
- Pategi and Edu flooded in all five events, marking a chronic flood core on
  the Niger floodplain.
- In April 2025 the flood centroid shifted to Kaiama, which took 166.6 km².

## Why it matters

The releases from the Jebba and Kainji hydroelectric dams are predictable in a
way that rainfall is not, so the communities downstream can be warned. Maps of
this kind support advance notification of planned releases, agricultural
compensation schemes and the placement of community early warning systems.
